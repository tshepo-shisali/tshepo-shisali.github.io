    SafeMate

    For anyone heading out the door

    Share a live trip with someone you trust, so they always know where you are. I built it with first dates in mind: the traveller decides what to share, from the route to who she's with, and nothing is shared by default.

    • Trip links that open in any browser, no app needed to follow along
    • SOS that sends photos and short audio clips to the people following
    • Opt-in alerts so nearby helpers can get there fast
    • One Flutter codebase for Android, iOS and the web, on Firebase

    A year and a half of solo work, now in app store review.
